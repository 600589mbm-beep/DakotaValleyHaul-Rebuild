Competitor names may appear only in neutral educational context when necessary. The site does not imitate competitor pages, publish competitor phone-number pages or imply affiliation.

## Implementation rules

1. One strong URL owns each distinct intent cluster.
2. Misspellings and “near me” variants are covered naturally in headings, FAQs and body copy rather than separate doorway pages.
3. Official city, county or state resources support current facility, hours and acceptance statements.
4. Public pricing remains limited to Dakota Valley’s $85 pickup minimum; complete quotes are confirmed from photos.
5. Hazardous waste, medication, sharps, asbestos and other regulated materials are clearly separated from ordinary junk-removal service.
6. All guide pages flow through the shared guide template, Article schema, FAQ schema, guide index, sitemap and internal-link system.
